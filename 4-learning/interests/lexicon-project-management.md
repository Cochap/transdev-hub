# Project management and continuous-improvement lexicon (branch 4)

EN / FR (common Quebec usage; most French terms are NOT confirmed by the OQLF dictionary). Sources: [PMI charter guide](https://www.pmi.org/blog/project-charter), [ASQ DMAIC](https://asq.org/quality-resources/dmaic), [ASQ SIPOC](https://asq.org/quality-resources/sipoc), [ASQ PDCA](https://asq.org/quality-resources/pdca-cycle), [LEI A3](https://www.lean.org/the-lean-post/articles/understanding-the-many-facets-of-the-a3/), [LEI gemba](https://www.lean.org/lexicon-terms/gemba/). Gathered 2026-11-04 for the DIX30 charter.

Key rule: the **charte de projet** comes right after the kickoff (short, high-level, signed by the sponsor); the detailed **énoncé de la portée** comes after, in planning.

| English | Français | Plain meaning | Stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Project charter | charte de projet | Authorizes the project and names the PM; sponsor signs | Initiation |
| Sponsor | commanditaire | Authorizes and funds the project | Initiation |
| Stakeholder register | registre des parties prenantes | Who is affected, their role and interest | Initiation |
| Scope statement | énoncé de la portée | Detailed in/out list, written after the charter | Planning |
| In / out of scope | dans / hors portée | The fence that stops scope creep | Charter + planning |
| SOW | énoncé des travaux | Work to be supplied, often by a vendor | Planning |
| Deliverable | livrable | Concrete result handed over | Planning |
| Milestone | jalon | Key checkpoint date with an owner | Charter |
| Assumption / constraint | hypothèse / contrainte | Taken as true / a hard limit | Charter |
| WBS | structure de répartition du travail | All work broken into small packages | Planning |
| RACI | matrice RACI | Who does, owns, is consulted, is informed | Planning |
| Risk register | registre des risques | Running list of risks and responses | Planning onward |
| SMART objective | objectif SMART | Specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, dated | Charter |
| KPI | indicateur clé de performance (ICP) | Measure of progress toward an objective | Charter |
| Baseline | situation de référence | The value before you change anything | Measure |
| Change control | gestion des changements | Formal yes/no on scope changes | Monitoring |
| Lessons learned | leçons apprises | What the team learned, captured at closing | Closing |
| A3 | rapport A3 | Lean one-pager: problem, analysis, countermeasures, plan | Lean alternative to a charter |
| SIPOC | SIPOC | Suppliers, inputs, process, outputs, customers | Define |
| DMAIC | DMAIC | Define, measure, analyze, improve, control | Six Sigma method |
| PDCA | PDCA (roue de Deming) | Plan, do, check, act | Lean cycle |
| VOC | voix du client | What satisfies or dissatisfies the customer | Define |
| Gemba | terrain | The real place where the work happens | Lean |
| Kaizen | amélioration continue | Small continuous improvements | Lean |

## Charter headings (use in this order)
Title and version · problem / business need · SMART objectives with KPIs and baselines · in / out of scope · deliverables · stakeholders and roles · assumptions and constraints · milestones · top risks · budget / resources · sponsor approval.
Pick an **A3** instead only for a small, single-problem improvement.
