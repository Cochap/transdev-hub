How a workshop gets its segment and its medal

The process runs in two parts. Part A answers which bar applies (segment S1, S2 or S3, from facts about the work and the staff). Part B answers how well the workshop clears that bar (bronze, silver or gold, from the audit). Each step below shows the Excel sheet that does it, the cells involved, and a real case from the workbooks.

The list comes from referential page 41. Rule in cell C10, read top-down:

  1. S3 if at least 2 of the 4 S3 jobs are done here and the S3 equipment is there.
  2. Otherwise S2 if at least 3 of the 5 S2 jobs and the S2 equipment.
  3. Otherwise S1.

In the example: 3 of 5 S2 jobs plus the S2 equipment, no S3 work. Base segment S2.

Cell C11 catches contradictions, for example “S3 work but no S3 equipment”. It shows « À vérifier » and the technical lead or auditor settles it on site.

Answer Oui only if the workshop's own technicians do it. Work sent to a supplier or another workshop counts as Non (row 13 of the form).

The staffing curve (referential p. 52) says how many buses one technician can normally look after. Older fleets need more technicians, so fewer buses each.

  • M, expected: 70 ÷ fleet age. A 7-year-old fleet gives 10 buses per technician.
  • × 0.8 factor: hard winter or more than 20 bus models means each technician can look after 20 % fewer buses. 10 expected becomes 8.
  • N, gap: actual vs expected. Over 20 % above normal means not enough technicians.
  • R, final: base segment minus one step. Never more than one step, never below S1.

C04 is C03 with 7 technicians instead of 12: 14.3 buses each, 43 % above normal. It drops from S2 to S1. When C04 hires up to 10 technicians (case C11), it goes back to S2.

Provisional values (yellow in Paramètres): the constant 70 and the 20 % tolerance are hypotheses. The 0.8 factor is Claude's, not the referential's. We change them once Pierre sends the real curve.

The 120 practices come from the referential audit (pp. 17–68). Each one has a Dès column: the first segment that must have it.

Ladders (x.1 / x.2 / x.3): one practice in three versions, one per segment. Only the version for your segment counts. Remplacée à partir de = the segment where the next version takes over.

The five ladders: 30 stock categories, 31 storage layout, 39 inventory counts, 81 organization, 82 manager role.

The lists are cumulative. Required practices: S1 = 50, S2 = 96, S3 = 110. Being S3 means meeting S2's list plus more.

For each pillar: practices in place ÷ practices required. Then two numbers decide the medal:

  • Total: 71 / 96 = 74 %.
  • Weakest pillar: Préparation at 54 %.

The rule checks gold first, then silver. The workshop needs both numbers over the line. Everyone below silver is bronze: bronze is the bottom group, not a prize. 74 % misses silver (75 %) by one point and 54 % is under the 60 % floor. Result: S2 bronze.

Gold is not 100 %: 90 % total with no pillar under 75 % is demanding but reachable.

The weakest-pillar floor matters because a strong pillar can't hide a weak one. One pillar at 20 % blocks the medal, even with a high total.

Safety gate (proposal, not in this example file): one safety practice below the bar means no medal at all, whatever the rest. The extract has no safety rows, so the gate does not run here (note in cell G11).

What is decided, and what is not

Staffing curve numbers

The constant 70, the 20 % tolerance and the 0.8 factor are placeholders. Waiting on Pierre's real curve; the message to him is drafted, not sent.

Medal thresholds

Proposal of Greg and Gabriel (11-03): gold 90 / 75, silver 75 / 60, bronze = everyone below silver. To confirm with Pierre.

Tension rule: two versions

The Excel uses the staffing curve only. The 10-30 proposal uses 2 of 3 ratios (buses per mechanic, % spare buses, buses per bay). Pick one before the call.

Yes/No vs levels 0–3

The medal file scores practices Oui/Non. The proposal scores each practice 0–3, with the bar rising per segment (S1 = 1, S2 = 2, S3 = 3), in grid copy …Maturity grid - v2 GR.xlsx. The grid's question count (67 vs 78) is still to redo.
