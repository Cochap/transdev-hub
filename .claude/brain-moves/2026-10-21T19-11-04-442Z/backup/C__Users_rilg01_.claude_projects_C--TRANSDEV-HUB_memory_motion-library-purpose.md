Greg's motion library (folder `C:\TRANSDEV HUB\motion-library`) exists so that an AI can research and pick motion and animation elements for his girlfriend Anouk's booking website (Divine Beauty by Anouk) and for video explainers. The sources he cares about are platforms centred on text animations, animated components and other motion, like reactbits.dev. He wants to point the AI at a platform's website or connect to its MCP so the AI does the research itself.

Why this is recorded: on 2026-10-01 and 2026-10-02 Greg had to correct me twice. First he said, "im confused if you even know what i created this motion library for", because the catalogue was a flat list of names that left out flagship sites. Then he said, "my request was specifically to figure out whether or not there were platforms centered on text animations, animated components, all the motion/animation stuff", after I had answered with design-inspiration tools such as Mobbin instead. Mobbin is the choice for UI layout and flow; it is not the answer to a motion question.

His selection principle: "you cant possibly crawl through more than a single platform exhaustively", so he wants ONE platform with a large enough catalogue that an AI can reach through an MCP, the way Mobbin is the single catalogue for UI. He values the MCP because it lets the AI search and parse a catalogue exhaustively.

How to apply:
- When Greg asks about motion, answer about motion and animation platforms, with text animations and animated components first. Do not substitute adjacent categories such as general design inspiration.
- Recommend one platform with a clear reason and at most one backup, not a long list of many sources.
- For each candidate state concretely whether it has an MCP, an llms.txt or a registry, what the AI actually gets back, and the real price or licence, verified at the source before stating it.
- Anouk's site follows the ZERO handoff (soft, smooth, relaxed motion, no bounce), so filter every motion recommendation against those rules.
