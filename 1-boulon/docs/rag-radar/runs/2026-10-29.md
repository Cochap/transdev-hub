## Trends (only from what was found)
- **Citations are becoming a built-in feature.** RAGFlow numbers them from an evidence registry, Dify keeps document metadata on them, Open WebUI shows sources under replies, and the PARCEL benchmark checks each claim against its source.
- **Tables and figures are the main open problem.** Docling and Unstructured shipped table fixes, Tessera and RouteDRAG are papers on it, and open models now retrieve page images directly (Nemotron 3.5, pplx-embed-v2-late). Most of this needs a GPU, and none of it has a French score.
- **Evaluation is moving into the platforms.** Copilot Studio and Dify both added traces and run comparison. The research (AGO) warns that AI judges need calibrating against human scores.
- **Vector databases now do keyword plus vector search in one store** (Qdrant BM25, Milvus Function Chain), so hybrid search no longer needs two systems.
