2 · WHERE IT STOPS

Three limits block the next load

The pilot ran on the free search tier. It is full, it cannot grow in place, and the paid tier needs an approval.

1 Storage is over the cap

cap 50 MBpilot 63.3 MB

The free tier allows 50 MB V. The pilot index reached 63.3 MB after the citation fields went in M. Writes have been blocked since 2026-10-06; reads still work.

Consequence: two large Vicinity manuals (99 MB and 23 MB) were never loaded. The full library cannot fit.

2 Free cannot upgrade in place

Free → Basic: noBasic → S1: yes

Microsoft: you cannot switch to or from Free V. The paid tier is a new service, so the index is rebuilt there and the agent is pointed at the new address.

Consequence: the move is a rebuild, not a toggle. A written cutover runbook already exists.

3 The approval is pending

ticket #77048sent 2026-09-25 · no reply

IT creates the paid search tier and a PDF-reading resource in Canada Central. Approver: Pierre Zivec.

Consequence: every step after this page waits on the ticket. Meanwhile only free checks run.

7 · DECIDE

One approval unlocks everything above

Back to where we started: 725 pages of 131,756, stopped by a free tier that is full.

The askCreate the paid Basic search tier in Canada Central.Ticket #77048. About CAD 112 a month for one partition.

A · Which brands first?

The three biggest by pages are Nova, New Flyer and MCI (112,275 pages, 85%). That is not the same as the three brands mechanics need first. Greg decides. Use chapter 5 to see the cost of each choice.

B · Which reader?

Layout is proven on tables, costs more. Content Understanding costs about half and is untested on our tables. A one-file trial on Basic settles it before any full load.

C · First free checks

While the ticket waits: count pages and text-layer coverage per manual, price each strategy, prepare the one-file trial. No spending, no changes to the live pilot.

Words used on this page

Tier: a size and price level of the Azure search service (Free, Basic, S1). Partition: a block of storage inside one service. Reader: the Azure service that reads PDF pages and tables. Chunk: one searchable piece of a manual. Vector: 1,536 numbers that capture a chunk's meaning. Re-ranker: a Microsoft model that re-sorts the top results by meaning. int8: a way to store each number in less space. Index: the searchable copy of the manuals. Golden set: questions with known answers used to score the system.
