When BOULON needs data or access from a colleague, prepare one direct request (full access plus the purpose), not a long list of detail questions the data will answer itself. Example: the 2026-09-29 message to [[alex-dumenne]] asking for read-only MIR access or a full export. Written in French ([[feedback-french-requests]]).
