## Boundaries stated or implied
- Not asked: full rewrite of the global reference, a garage investment programme, changes to HSE obligations, awarding official certifications, a database or integration for the ID card. Not started without an explicit widening of the mandate.
- The first calculation does not freely change input families; extra needs are logged as questions or proposed changes.

## Confidentiality note (D11)
The meeting stressed the method is confidential and unpublished. A "C2" level was mentioned; its official meaning is unconfirmed. This wiki stays local. What counts as an "external AI tool" and which tools are authorised is unanswered (Q10 in [[open-questions]]); [[greg-riley]] should confirm with [[henri]] or [[pierre-zivec]]. The debrief itself was drafted with ChatGPT from the transcripts, which is why Q10 should be answered before more material goes to such tools.

## Proposed (not decided) controls from the debrief writer
Keep for each workshop: calculated score, retained segment, justification, who arbitrated, method version. Check SEG01 to SEG08 (inputs unchanged, ratios defined, weights table kept, interdependent factors tested, human review kept, results logged, maturity separated, French comparison prepared). These are PM recommendations, not Transdev decisions.
