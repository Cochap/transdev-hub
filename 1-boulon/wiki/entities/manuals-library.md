**Bottom line:** Transdev Quebec's manual library is 3,052 PDF/DOC files totalling 6.95 GB (measured 2026-09-23), far more than the "100+ manuals" first assumed; only a core of about 150 to 300 files needs to be searchable in BOULON first.

Plain words: this is the raw pile of manuals in `OneDrive - TRANSDEV\Manuels` that [[boulon]] is meant to answer from. Claude read only file names, folders and sizes, not contents.

## Size stats
- 3,052 files, 6.95 GB.
- 101 files over 16 MB (about 4.8 GB): too big for Free or Basic to read whole ([[tier-limits]]). 4 files over 128 MB (the S1 limit).
- About 1,300 files are CD-ROM page fragments (MCI `pm55570`, `Dseries\parts`).
- Confirmed duplicates: Delphi connector catalogue (twice), Lion gen2 electrical schematics (twice), 2019 Vicinity/Grande West parts manual (twice), and "B21KLS-1 Parts Manual - Copie".

## By type (estimates from names)
Service/maintenance about 150 files (2.0 GB); parts catalogues about 250 (1.8 GB); supplier manuals (ZF, Voith, Delphi, Cummins) about 400 (1.2 GB); electrical schematics about 60 (0.8 GB); operator manuals about 40 (0.5 GB); CD-ROM fragments about 1,300 (0.35 GB); duplicates 5+ (0.18 GB+).

## Vehicle families seen in the library
Nova (labelled "Transdev Québec" assumed current, "Véolia" assumed older since Veolia Transport was the company's earlier name), New Flyer (SR2254, SR2300, SR1865 assumed current; also SR1259 parts/service/operator files), Lion, Vicinity, Grande West, MCI, Prevost, Orion, IC. Which are still in service is unknown; Greg's fleet list is needed.

## Proposed core library
About 150 to 300 files, 2.5 to 3.5 GB, about 20,000 pages (range 10,000 to 40,000, a guess that can be off by 3 times for scanned PDFs). 34 core files exceed 16 MB; 2 (Nova LB90 and LB52 service manuals, 0.28 GB) exceed 128 MB. Later group about 900 files; never group about 1,400+ (fragments, duplicates, DVD or audio).

## What the pilot indexed
Only 23 of 25 uploaded PDFs, 2,250 chunks ([[azure-search-index]]); no Orion manual.
