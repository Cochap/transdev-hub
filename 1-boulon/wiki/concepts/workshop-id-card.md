**Bottom line:** a printable sign at the entrance of each garage listing the workshop's main characteristics. Manual entry, stable layout, familiar tool (PowerPoint preferred, final choice to confirm). First deliverable (L1) of [[workshop-segmentation]].

## What it is
A panel printed (possibly laminated) and displayed at the garage entrance. Generated occasionally, such as when an operation starts or all cards are prepared. Ease of use and layout stability matter more than automation. A free Word template was discouraged (layout drift, overwriting the template); locked Word fields or a PowerPoint mask were considered, PowerPoint preferred. The reference format must be respected; a more Canadian bus illustration is allowed at a similar size. No database, automatic collection or custom app was requested. Source: [[workshop-segmentation-debrief-v0-1]].

## Fields named in the meeting
Identification (location, segment, level in segment); contract (type, duration, end date); operating conditions (normal or severe); fleet owner; number of buses; spare-vehicle share; average annual km per bus; average age; number of different models; shortest inspection cycle; energy mix; technicians and other staff; shifts; building owner; bays and floor area (square feet and/or metres still to decide); parking spaces. Compare with the official template before treating this as complete. Display fields are not all calculation factors.

## Current build
A web app prototype exists in `2-projects/workshop-id-card/` (built the same day in a separate session; see its ROUTER.md). The meeting said manual entry and did not ask for a custom app, so whether the web app replaces or complements the PowerPoint is a decision for Greg and Henri (open).
