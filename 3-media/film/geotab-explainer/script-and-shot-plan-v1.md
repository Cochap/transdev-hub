## Script (~180 words, about 58 s at 190 wpm)
1. You think a bus breaks down without warning. It doesn't.
2. Every bus is already talking: speed, braking, seat belts, engine codes. All day long.
3. The thing that listens is called Geotab.
4. It started in 2000 in Oakville, Ontario [V1], as a small box plugged into a vehicle.
5. It watches four things: where the bus is, how it's driven, how the engine feels, and when it needs service.
6. So how does it work?
7. A device in each bus sends its data to one live map.
8. Speeding, harsh braking, or no seat belt? The driver gets AI coaching.
9. An engine fault code? A work order lands in the mechanics' queue, and reminders roll in before the next service.
10. At Transdev Canada, dispatchers see the whole fleet at once [V2], and one rule reaches every bus in one move.
11. Mechanics fix the bus before it fails, not after.
12. So that bus that broke down "without warning"? It was warning us the whole time. Now someone is listening.
13. What would your bus tell you today?
